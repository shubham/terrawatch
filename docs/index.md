TerraWatch
==========

TerraWatch is a live earthquake monitor for Android — a global map, a digest of earthquake alerts
(never framed as early warning), a full history archive, and trend insights, built on public USGS
and EMSC seismic data.

This site hosts TerraWatch's [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) and its `app-ads.txt` (for AdMob
publisher verification), as required for the app's Google Play Store listing.

- [Privacy Policy](privacy.html)
- Source: this repository
- Contact / support: open an issue on this repository

TerraWatch does not predict earthquakes and is not a substitute for official emergency alerts,
seismic early-warning systems, or guidance from local authorities.
